The running example

Reducing time-to-hire across a multi-region operation

One realistic HR scenario threads through every module — regression on what drives time-to-hire, ANOVA across regions, a Gage R&R on interview scoring, DOE on the hiring process, and SPC to hold the gains. Every dataset is real and answer-keyed.
