Black Belt Practice Datasets

Realistic HR-themed CSVs for the exercises, all on the course's running example (time-to-hire / onboarding across a multi-region operation). Every answer below was computed with numpy/scipy/statsmodels — the full key is in ANSWER-KEYS.md. Open the CSVs in a spreadsheet, jamovi/JASP, Python, or R.

FileUsed inWhat's insideKey verified answers
time-to-hire-regression.csvMod 08 (regression)120 hires: interview_rounds, offer_delay_days, recruiter_caseload → time_to_hire_daysR² = 0.661; +1 interview round ≈ +3.45 days; all predictors significant
time-to-hire-by-region.csvMod 07 (ANOVA)time_to_hire_days for 4 regions (30 each)means 24.5 / 26.8 / 30.0 / 32.2; F = 15.0, p = 2.5e-08 (regions differ)
interview-score-gagerr.csvMod 04 (MSA)3 interviewers × 10 candidates × 2 ratingsGage R&R %study = 30.9%, ndc = 4 → marginal measurement system
onboarding-capability.csvMod 05 (capability)100 onboarding completion times (days), USL = 20μ = 14.01, σ = 1.89, Cpk = 1.05; Shapiro p = 0.039 (borderline normal)
response-time-skewed.csvMod 05 (non-normal)100 first-response times (hours), right-skewedShapiro p < 0.0001 (not normal); after log transform p = 0.664
doe-factorial.csvMod 10 (DOE)2³ full factorial, 2 replicates (A guide, B pre-screen, C tool)effects A −6.34, B −2.74, C −1.48, A×B +1.43 (all significant); R² = 0.963
before-after-tth.csvMod 07 (t-test)time-to-hire before vs after a change (25 each)before 31.0, after 26.6; t = 2.88, p = 0.0061 (significant)
offer-acceptance-by-region.csvMod 07 (chi-square)accepted vs declined offers, 4 regionsχ² = 9.18, dof = 3, p = 0.027 (acceptance depends on region)
tth-subgroups.csvMod 13 (control)20 subgroups of 5 (weekly time-to-hire samples)X̿ = 26.20, R̄ = 4.57; X̄ UCL 28.84 / LCL 23.57; subgroup 15 out of control
