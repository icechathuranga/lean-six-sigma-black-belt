# Resources — Black Belt

Links checked November 2026. Black Belt is statistics-heavy, so the big addition versus Green Belt is a **free statistics tool** — a spreadsheet alone gets painful past basic tests.

## 🧮 Free statistics tools (pick one)

- **jamovi** — free, open-source, point-and-click (feels like SPSS). Does t-tests, ANOVA, regression, and more with no code. Great first choice. → https://www.jamovi.org/download.html
- **JASP** — free, open-source, similar to jamovi; excellent output and clear menus. → https://jasp-stats.org/download/
- **Python** — `pandas` + `scipy` + `statsmodels` (+ `pingouin`) cover everything in this course, including DOE. Free. → https://www.python.org/
- **R** + **RStudio** — the statistician's standard; free, enormous package ecosystem. → https://www.r-project.org/
- A **spreadsheet** (Excel / Google Sheets / LibreOffice Calc) still handles capability, control limits, basic t-tests and simple regression.

*(Minitab and JMP are the industry-standard paid tools; both offer free trials if you want to try them.)*

## 📋 The exam bodies of knowledge (free, authoritative)

Download these to see the exact scope a Black Belt is expected to know:
- **ASQ — Certified Six Sigma Black Belt (CSSBB)** — the Body of Knowledge is a free PDF on the certification page. → https://asq.org/cert/six-sigma
- **IASSC — Lean Six Sigma Black Belt** — vendor-neutral Body of Knowledge on their site. → https://iassc.org/

## 🎓 Free study & practice

- **Six Sigma Study Guide** — free Black Belt articles and short practice exams (the full question bank is paid). → https://sixsigmastudyguide.com/free/
- **Khan Academy — Statistics & Probability** — free, superb for shoring up the foundations behind Modules 6–8. → https://www.khanacademy.org/math/statistics-probability

## 📖 Books (optional, but excellent)

- **"The Six Sigma Handbook"** — Pyzdek & Keller. The comprehensive desk reference for Black Belts.
- **"Design and Analysis of Experiments"** — Douglas Montgomery. *The* classic on DOE (Modules 10–11); deep but authoritative.
- **"The ASQ Certified Six Sigma Black Belt Study Guide"** — if you later decide to sit the ASQ exam.

## 🏅 Certification (optional)

You said the goal is the skills, not a certificate — but if you ever want one, the two most recognised Black Belts are **IASSC** (exam-only) and **ASQ CSSBB** (which expects completed project experience). Your course skills map directly onto both bodies of knowledge; add the free BoK PDFs above plus exam practice.
