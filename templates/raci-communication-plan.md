# RACI & Communication Plan — [Project]

> Clarify who does what, and keep stakeholders informed. Module 02 & 03.

## RACI — one **A** (Accountable) per activity, at least one **R**
R = Responsible (does it) · A = Accountable (owns it) · C = Consulted (two-way) · I = Informed (one-way)

| Activity / deliverable | Champion | Black Belt | Process owner | Team | HR Ops | IT |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Approve charter | A | R | C | I | I | I |
| Collect data | I | A | R | R | C | C |
| Run analysis | I | R/A | C | C | I | I |
| Pilot solution | A | R | R | R | C | C |
| Hand over control plan | I | R | A | C | I | I |

*Check: every row has exactly one A and at least one R.*

## Communication plan
| Audience | What they need to hear | Channel | Frequency | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sponsor / steering | Progress vs tollgates, risks, $ | 1-page / meeting | Fortnightly | Black Belt |
| Process team | Changes, actions, wins | Stand-up / board | Weekly | Process owner |
| Affected staff | What changes & why | Email / briefing | At each milestone | Champion |
| Finance | Validated savings | Report | At close | Black Belt |
